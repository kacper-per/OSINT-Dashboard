Limitations

Public data may be stale, incomplete, redacted, or misleading. Missing results do not prove that an asset or control is absent. HTTP checks are limited to discovered hostnames and do not test vulnerabilities. The tool intentionally avoids aggressive crawling, port scanning, authentication, exploitation, and paid data sources.

Ethical and Legal Note

This tool performs passive OSINT reconnaissance using public sources and basic non-invasive HTTP checks. It does not exploit vulnerabilities, brute-force credentials, attack systems, bypass authentication, or perform aggressive scanning. Use it only for educational purposes, authorized assessments, or domains owned or approved by the user.
